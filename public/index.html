Taiwanese Poker

Split 7 cards into a 1-card, 2-card, and 4-card PLO hand. Two boards run. Everything gets compared.

Solo mode — no room needed. You get a random 7-card hand and try different ways to split it. Each split is scored against 150 independent board runouts, each against an opponent hand drawn from a pool pre-solved by iterative best-response simulation (see the strategy research in this project's README) — a strong, empirically near-optimal opponent, not a random one. Try a split, see its average EV, tweak it, and compare. Standard scoring only (no homerun).

How this variant works
  • Each player gets 7 cards and splits them into a 1-card, 2-card, and 4-card hand (using all 7).
  • Two separate 5-card boards run out.
  • Each hand combines with each board to make the best 5-card poker hand: the 1-card and 2-card hands are "best of the pool" (hold'em style — even the board alone can play), the 4-card hand follows standard PLO rules (exactly 2 of your 4 cards + exactly 3 board cards).
  • Every player is compared head-to-head against every other player, per board, per hand type. Winning the 1-card comparison is worth 1 point, 2-card is worth 2, 4-card (PLO) is worth 3 — per board.
  • Exact ties push (no points change hands).
